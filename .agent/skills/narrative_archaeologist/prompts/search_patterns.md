# 搜索策略模板 (Search Strategy Patterns)

> **Core Philosophy**: 不搜“怎么做” (How)，只搜“是什么” (What/Nature) 和 “谁说过” (Who)。

## 1. 哲学桥接 (Philosophical Bridge)
*   `"{Technical Term}" philosophy metaphor`
*   `"{Technical Term}" artistic interpretation`
*   `"spiritual meaning of {Technical Term}"`
*   *Example*: `"Room Tone" philosophy "ghost of a room"`

## 2. 跨学科隐喻 (Interdisciplinary Metaphor)
*   `"{Technical Term}" in nature examples`
*   `"{Technical Term}" vs biology/architecture/quantum physics`
*   `"history of {Technical Term}" origin story`
*   *Example*: `"Pink Noise" in nature examples biological rhythms`

## 3. 错误与伪影 (Artifacts as Features)
*   `"{Artifact Name}" origin cause history`
*   `"why does {Technical Term} sound like {Description}"`
*   *Example*: `"Musical Noise" spectral subtraction origin bell labs`

## 4. 科学/艺术原本 (Primary Source Verification)
*   `"{Quote Segment}" authentic source`
*   `"Did {Person} actually say {Quote}?"`
*   `"{Event} real history debunked"`
*   *Example*: `"John Cage anechoic chamber Harvard 1951 story"`
